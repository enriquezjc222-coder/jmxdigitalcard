JMX DIGITAL CARD — ADMIN GOOGLE AUTH UPDATE

- Added Continue with Google to the administrator/editor sign-in screen.
- Existing email/password sign-in remains available as a fallback.
- Google sign-in does NOT grant administrator rights by itself.
- The authenticated Firebase UID must still match platform/config.adminUid to receive JMX administrator access.
- Unauthorized Google accounts are signed out and rejected.
- Existing client Google authentication, inventory, analytics, card data, URLs, and Firestore rules were preserved.

Firebase requirement:
Authentication > Sign-in method > Google must be enabled, and the production domain must be an Authorized domain.
