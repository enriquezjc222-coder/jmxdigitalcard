JMX Digital Card - Feature Control Center restored

Restored administrator module switches in dashboard.html/dashboard.js.

Controls:
- GLOBAL / All Plans: OFF hides a module for both Basic and Premium public cards.
- BASIC: controls which modules Basic public cards can display.
- PREMIUM: controls which modules Premium public cards can display.

Modules include description, save contact, quick actions, phone, second phone, WhatsApp, email, website, location, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, services, gallery, video, QR, final CTA, business links, catalog/PDF, and custom business link.

Existing behavior is preserved by default:
- Premium modules default ON.
- Basic defaults remain the same as the previous plan restrictions until changed by the administrator.
- No existing card, inventory, owner, claim, profile, or analytics records are deleted by this update.

Google administrator sign-in is also available on the platform dashboard and remains protected by platform/config adminUid.
