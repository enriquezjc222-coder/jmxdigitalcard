JMX DIGITAL CARD - GOOGLE AUTH UPDATE - AUG 2026

Added Google authentication as the primary activation and owner-login option.

Files changed:
- activate.html
- activate.js
- login.html
- login.js
- portal.css

Activation flow:
1. Customer opens an available/sold NFC card URL.
2. Customer enters the private activation code when required.
3. Customer clicks Continue with Google and chooses a Google account.
4. The selected Firebase-authenticated Google account becomes the card owner.
5. The card status changes to activated and the profile editor opens.

Email/password activation remains available as a fallback.
Owner Login also supports Continue with Google, which is required so customers who activated with Google can return later without needing an email/password account.

IMPORTANT FIREBASE CONSOLE SETUP:
Authentication > Sign-in method > Google must be enabled for the jmx-digital-card Firebase project.
The production domain jmxdigitalcard.com must be listed under Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains.
