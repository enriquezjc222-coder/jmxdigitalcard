IMPORTANT ABOUT AUTOMATIC STRIPE BILLING:
The UI is prepared, but automatic activation/deactivation after payment MUST be performed by a trusted server/webhook, not browser JavaScript. Do not place a Stripe secret key in HTML/JS. Configure the checkout URL in Dashboard > Platform. When Stripe is launched, connect its webhook to update cards/{cardId}.subscription and cards/{cardId}.plan. Complimentary Premium always overrides billing entitlement.

Suggested webhook rules:
checkout/subscription active -> plan Premium, subscription.status active, subscription.source stripe
subscription canceled/unpaid -> plan Basic, subscription.status canceled/unpaid, unless complimentaryPremium=true
complimentaryPremium=true -> effective plan Premium regardless of billing
