JMX DIGITAL CARD - CLIENT ANALYTICS & INVENTORY UPDATE

Added to the platform administrator dashboard:
- Compact Basic and Premium client lists instead of oversized client cards.
- Click any client row to open a large modal with full client/card details.
- Modal closes with X or by clicking outside it.
- Per-client analytics: total profile opens, current-month opens, total clicks, current-month clicks, and click breakdown by Phone, WhatsApp, Email, Website, social networks, Save Contact, Share, etc.
- Copy / Recover URL button for replacement NFC cards. The permanent URL is not changed.
- Edit Profile, Change Plan, Suspend/Reactivate from the client modal.
- Release / Reset for Reuse for non-protected cards only. This archives the prior client/profile/analytics, unlinks ownership, clears old profile/media, resets analytics, creates a new activation code, and returns the same permanent URL to Available inventory.
- BOSS is explicitly protected from delete, regenerate, or release/reset operations.
- Available/Unclaimed inventory remains separate and shows permanent URL, plan, physical type, NFC state, activation code, created date and controls.
