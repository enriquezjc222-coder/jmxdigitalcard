IMPORTANT
Static-token authorization is NOT a cryptographic guarantee against cloning standard NFC memory tags. A complete copy of the NDEF URL also copies the static token. Strong anti-clone requires cryptographic/dynamic NFC hardware (for example NTAG 424 DNA/SUN) or equivalent server-verifiable dynamic authentication.
